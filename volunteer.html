Volunteer with us

The festival runs on volunteers. A few hours of your time, before the day or on it, makes the difference between a good festival and a great one.

Setup and clear downMarquees, seating, signage, stalls and the site plan, the morning before and the evening after.
StewardingWelcoming guests, keeping performance areas clear and pointing people to what they need.
Welcome deskChecking registrations, handing out brochures and answering questions.
Children's areaGames, craft and cultural activity for younger guests. Suitable checks are arranged where needed.
Media and photographyPhotography, video, livestream support and social media on the day.
Planning and fundraisingYear round help with logistics, sponsorship, the brochure and community outreach.

Manchester IgboIriji Festival

The Manchester Igbo Iriji Festival is the annual Igbo New Yam Festival held in Manchester, United Kingdom. A day of thanksgiving, ceremony, music, dance and food for the Igbo community and everyone who wants to celebrate with us.
