Artwork & Submission Guidelines

For Advert & Photo Submissions

Send your pictures or completed Ad Artwork to igbomanchester@gmail.com. Please use your Name-Iri ji as email subject reference.

Message with Photos: If you want family members' names displayed in the brochure, list the names of everyone in the picture from left to right.
Multiple Pictures: Save picture files with clear numbers, e.g. Main (for main picture), family_pic_1, family_pic_2.
Photo Descriptions: In your message, specify photo names: e.g. "family_pic_1 from left to right: Emeka, Chioma, Izuchukwu".

Manchester IgboIriji Festival
