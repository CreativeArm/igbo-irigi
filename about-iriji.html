About · About Iriji

What Iriji means

Iriji, the Igbo New Yam Festival, is one of the oldest and most widely kept celebrations in Igbo land. It gives thanks for the harvest and opens the new season of eating yam.

The Meaning

Iri ji: to eat yam

The name comes from two Igbo words, iri, to eat, and ji, yam. Before the festival no new yam is eaten. The oldest man in the community, or the invited elder, offers prayers of thanks to Chukwu and to the ancestors, breaks the first yam, tastes it and shares it. Only then does the harvest belong to everyone.

Yam is not simply food. Growing it takes a full year of labour, and a good yam harvest has always been the measure of a careful farmer and a stable household. That is why yam is called the king of crops, and why the day of its release is treated with ceremony rather than casually.

The blessing is followed by celebration: drumming, dance, masquerade, new clothes, visiting, and food shared across families. Gratitude comes first, then joy.

Why we celebrate

Four things the festival carries

ThanksgivingThe harvest is received as a gift, not an achievement. The day begins with prayer and with elders speaking on behalf of the community.
Harvest and labourYam takes a year in the ground. Iriji honours the work of farming and, in the diaspora, the work that keeps families going.
Family and communityUmunna, the kindred, gathers. Quarrels are settled, visitors are fed, and the wider community is invited in.
GenerationsChildren see the ceremony performed properly, hear Igbo spoken widely, and learn the customs by taking part in them.
Iriji in Manchester

The same ceremony, a different city

Manchester has one of the largest Igbo populations in the north of England. Town unions, women's groups, youth groups and church communities all keep their own calendars, and Iriji is the day they meet as one. The ceremony is kept faithfully: elders, prayers, the blessing and sharing of yam. Around it sits a full festival day of cultural performance, traders, food and family activity.

Manchester IgboIriji Festival

The Manchester Igbo Iriji Festival is the annual Igbo New Yam Festival held in Manchester, United Kingdom. A day of thanksgiving, ceremony, music, dance and food for the Igbo community and everyone who wants to celebrate with us.
