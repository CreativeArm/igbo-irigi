Frequently asked questions

If your question is not answered here, the committee is happy to help.

Iriji is the Igbo New Yam Festival. It marks the end of the farming season and the point at which the first yams of the harvest may be eaten. Elders give thanks, bless the new yam and share it with the community before the celebration begins.

The date is set by the committee each year and published here as soon as it is confirmed. The provisional date for the next festival is shown on the home page.

The festival is held in Manchester, United Kingdom. The venue is confirmed each year and published on the Venue and Travel page with directions, parking and public transport information.

Registration is not a ticket, it helps us plan for numbers, seating and catering. Please register yourself and anyone coming with you on the Register page.

Admission arrangements are confirmed each year. Where entry is free it is made possible by sponsors, advertisers and donations from the community.

Yes. Iriji is a family day and children are part of the reason we hold it. Please keep children with a responsible adult at all times.

Sponsorship packages run from community partner to headline sponsor, and can be tailored. See the Sponsor Us page or contact the committee to discuss.

You can advertise in the festival brochure and on site. Sizes, deadlines and rates are set each year and listed on the Advertise page.

Volunteers help with setup, stewarding, the welcome desk, the children's area, media and clear down. Tell us your availability on the Volunteer page.

Yes. The ceremony and main stage are streamed so that family and friends who cannot travel can watch. The Live Stream page carries the player on the day.

Manchester IgboIriji Festival

The Manchester Igbo Iriji Festival is the annual Igbo New Yam Festival held in Manchester, United Kingdom. A day of thanksgiving, ceremony, music, dance and food for the Igbo community and everyone who wants to celebrate with us.
