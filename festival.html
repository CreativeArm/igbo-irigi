What to expect

A full day, built around the ceremony

The Iriji ceremony sits at the centre of the day. Around it there is a cultural market, food from local traders, performances from town unions and cultural troupes, masquerade display, a children's area and live music into the evening.

Traditional dress is encouraged. Many groups wear matching cloth.
Bring seating if you prefer your own chair, and dress for Manchester weather.
Card and cash are both usually accepted by traders, but bring some cash.
The ceremony and main stage are livestreamed for family who cannot travel.

Manchester IgboIriji Festival

The Manchester Igbo Iriji Festival is the annual Igbo New Yam Festival held in Manchester, United Kingdom. A day of thanksgiving, ceremony, music, dance and food for the Igbo community and everyone who wants to celebrate with us.
